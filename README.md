  ❧   About

    I am a graduate in Electrical Engineering from the National Institute of Technology Karnataka, Surathkal (Class of 2025). My work exists at the intersection of classical engineering discipline and modern machine intelligence — designing systems that are not only technically sound but deeply purposeful.

    I focus on applied AI, time-series intelligence, and scalable ML systems. I am particularly interested in building infrastructure-level tools — systems that operate quietly, reliably, and efficiently beneath the surface.

    Outside engineering, I write long-form reflections on Medium, create science & space content on YouTube, play chess, and explore the hills and coastlines of Karnataka.
